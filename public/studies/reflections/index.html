What changed

The stronger option narrows the reflection spread, adds a clearer varnish/resin coating and raises the reflection contribution on wood and dice. It preserves the same albedo, normals, roughness images, scratches and grime. Chip clay, padding and felt keep their finishes.

Stronger is now used when Local reflections is On. Effects Off restores the original finish exactly. High polish is a comparison-only alternative with more pronounced highlights. The HDR environment and cached probe resolution/position stay the same for every On capture.
