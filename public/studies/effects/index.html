What these switches actually do

Off already has the casino HDR lighting, worn materials and soft shadows. Local reflections add a cached view of the table to walnut, dice and metal; they do not add new chip scratches. Contact shadows tighten the existing shadow map. Lens flares need an actual casino lamp in view. The downward material shot has none; the lamp-facing shot has one. No extra light, exposure boost or edited material is added to the On image.

The lamp pair uses a clearer optical halo and ghosts, still attached to the actual HDR lamp and blocked by geometry. All optional effects now offer measured Auto and explicit On/Off in the game.
