# Table audio

Open the gear on the TV display, choose **Table audio**, and press **Test effects** to hear a sample throw and chip landing. Dice and chip effects start at 75%. Room ambience and music start off. Each display saves its own mix. The announcer has its own setting.

Browsers that block autoplay need a click, tap, or key press on the TV display. The Craps Night launcher already allows autoplay in its dedicated browser.
